About RUDRA

RUDRA Fitness Centre was founded with one mission: to make fitness accessible, enjoyable, and effective for everyone. Whether you're just starting your fitness journey or you're a seasoned athlete, our facility and team are here to support you every step of the way.

We combine modern equipment, expert coaching, and a welcoming community to help you reach your goals - faster and safer.

RUDRA Fitness Centre is owned and managed by Prashant Misra, dedicated to building a stronger, healthier community in Tarajivanpur.
